# Quick task 261023-u13: the note page as a gist console

Every note now opens on a rail plus a large gist, replacing the earlier "annotated" layout. The note lifting (including new explore-note lifting) stays behind the handler boundary.

## What the page shows now

- **Dense quick note** (for example "Crop images and profile photos"): a sticky left rail holds the label "Note", the title made from the file name, a chip saying where the note went (Shipped, Todo open, Untraced or Still a note), one big number (the count of things it says after the gist, with a line such as "1 decided · 1 ask · 1 problem · 3 limits"), a stat list (Noted, Age, Promoted, Todo, Note → shipped, Mentions, Words), the todo card, then copy path and View / Source. Beside it the first sentence is said large, with a line under it ("Became a todo and shipped 22 Oct 2026."). Then a Lifted | As written toggle and a "What it says" list with one numbered row per clause, then Mentions, then one "Also in this file" row of chips.
- **One-line note:** the big number is 1 with "line, nothing else to lift" and "11 words · Became a todo, still open." There is no toggle. The list is replaced by "That's the whole note: one line." (plus "It hasn't been promoted to a todo." when it is still a note), followed by a "Where it went" list that holds the todo card.
- **Untraced or unpromoted note:** the chip reads Untraced or Still a note, the stat list says Promoted: No in the missing colour where it applies, and the card keeps the earlier "Promoted, but untraced" or "Still a note" text.
- **Explore note:** the rail shows "Explore note", the title, its context, an "N sections" chip, the big number (measured figures, or points made when there are none) with "15 points across 3 sections · 5 calls flagged", and a stat list (Noted, Sections, Points, Tables, Decided, Open / blocked, Lines). The gist is the note's lede, or its one-sentence shape when there is none. Below is a "Section by section" list: each section's heading, key sentence, up to four bold-led items (with "+N more"), up to three flagged calls, up to five figure chips, its shape on the side, and "Read +" that opens the section as the app renders it, in place ("Fold –" closes it). As written shows the whole document.

## Decisions made as built

- Explore notes keep the Lifted | As written toggle (the sketch gave them none), so the whole document is one click away besides Source.
- "Read +" shows the app's own rendering of the section, cut from the page's sanitized document at heading boundaries. Sections are matched to headings by order when the counts agree, otherwise by heading text; an unmatched section has no Read +.
- Where it went: a first "Became a todo" that is done reads Shipped, otherwise Todo open; with no such todo a note marked promoted is Untraced and any other is Still a note. An explore note's chip is "N sections", in the open colour when any todo points at it.
- Age compares the note's written date and time (read literally) with the clock; Note → shipped is the day count between the note's date and the first dated "(YYYY-MM-DD)" in the todo's resolution, plus one. The Promoted, Todo and Note → shipped rows appear only when there is something to say.
- Singular forms are used ("1 section", "1 point made", "1 call flagged").
- The index number on a row takes the label's colour, else its group's (decided green, problem in-flight, open missing). So "Suggested" gets an in-flight number because its label is in-flight, as the sketch does.
- Wiki links like `[[artifact-chrome-drift]]` in explore text are shown as code chips (the shared inline renderer would otherwise print the brackets).
- The commits are three instead of one per plan-task boundary: the lifting layer, the page, then the browser test, capture script and design-language record.

## What the lifting reads now

A quick note's rows carry their group, their first sentence and the rest, plus the note's word count, the file-name title and the file's line count; an Open question label takes the missing colour. An explore note yields its lede (or a "Captured from…" origin line), a one-sentence shape, its `##` sections (heading, short heading, key sentence and where it came from, items, table rows, asides, subsections, flagged calls, figures, side shape), the unique figures, flag totals, points and tables. Values match the sketch's own model for all twelve sketch notes (checked by running both over every one). The lifting is bounded: at most 40 sections, 4 items, 8 flags and 12 figures per section, and a 1 MB body with a 200k-character line lifts in well under 250 ms as a quick and as an explore note.
