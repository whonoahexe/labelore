<objective>
Build sketch 016's winner, **A: Run sheet**, as the VALIDATION page view. A VALIDATION.md is a phase's test contract. Its frontmatter carries status, nyquist_compliant, wave_0_complete, created and sometimes reconciled or updated. The body holds Test Infrastructure, Sampling Rate, a Per-Task Verification Map (up to 10 columns, 33 rows on the hard doc), Wave 0 Requirements, Manual-Only Verifications and the Validation Sign-Off. The view answers "how will this phase be proven, and how far along is that?" at a glance. It opens on the status, the dims and three verdict cells. Then come how it's tested, the map as wave lanes with a sticky inspector, a quiet Wave 0, the human-check cards and the sign-off.

Purpose: VALIDATION is the next type in the per-type view loop (DISCUSSION-LOG, CONTEXT, RESEARCH, PATTERNS, UI-SPEC and UAT are done). Today it renders through the generic promoted-section view, which shows the map as a raw 10-column table.

Output: a server-side VALIDATION extractor and handler, a pure composer, and the view components and CSS. They are wired through the view registry seam exactly as UAT is, but every shared-file edit sits at a VALIDATION-only anchor (see merge_anchors) so the two sibling batch items merge without conflicts. Also: unit, corpus, contract and static-markup tests; a narrow e2e spec for post-merge; and gsd-browser side-by-side evidence against the sketch, with every deviation fixed or recorded.
</objective>
