<objective>
Build sketch 021's winner, **E: Board + ledger** (with its round-3 refinements), as the VERIFICATION page view. A VERIFICATION.md is the verifier's verdict on a phase or quick task. Today it renders through the sketch-004 B3 folded-chapters layout (`src/web/views/layout-verification.ts`), which reads the truths table and the frontmatter but buries the verdict, never ties a gap to the plan task that closes it, and shows truths and requirements as tables. Purpose: VERIFICATION is the next type in the per-type view loop (DISCUSSION-LOG, CONTEXT, RESEARCH, PATTERNS, UI-SPEC, UAT, VALIDATION, SECURITY, UI-REVIEW, PLAN and SUMMARY are built). It must degrade, never throw, across the five shapes the sketch proved: the template (synthetic), frontmatter gaps with a prose closure shape (studio-portal 04), human_needed with mixed truth states (sideby 04), a re-verification with data-blocked items (deid 01), and passed with behavior-unverified truths (labelore 05).
