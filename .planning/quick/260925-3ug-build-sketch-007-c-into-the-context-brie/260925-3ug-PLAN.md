<objective>
Build the winning variant C from sketch 007 into the CONTEXT brief's ideas block (`.view-context-ideas`). Specific ideas become one column of items tagged RULE / LEANING / NOTE, each with its bold lead and full text. Deferred ideas are grouped by what happened to them: handed on, declined, passed over, out of scope, carried forward, and a default Parked group. Output: parser extraction (kind, fate, from, dest, revisit), composer groups, the variant-C view and CSS, a design-language doc entry, unit, corpus and e2e tests, regenerated goldens, and screenshots of SP 01 and SP 03.
