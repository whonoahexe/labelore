### The two requirements the previous audit downgraded

**TGT-08** — *"Every view shows when its data was read from disk, and a Refresh action re-reads
the project."* Both clauses now hold.

`src/server/index.ts:235-241` branches on `snapshot.loadStatus.status !== 'ok'` and returns
`{refreshed: false, error}` with HTTP 500 **before** the `derived = buildDerivedViews(snapshot)`
swap on line 249. A failed read can no longer report success, and can no longer replace the good
bundle server-wide. Verified live during execution, not just under test doubles: with `.planning`
renamed away mid-session, `POST /api/refresh` returned 500 `refreshed:false`, and the immediately
following `GET /api/presentation` still served the pre-refresh `readAt` and `GET /api/tree` still
served the full retained tree; restoring `.planning` and refreshing again recovered with a new
`readAt`.

`globals.css:1487-1496` replaces the old `display: none` with a compact treatment under 62rem —
the decorative icon and "Snapshot read" label drop out, the timestamp and Refresh button stay
rendered and operable. `.shell-controls` was relaid out from an absolutely positioned overlay to
a normal full-width flowed row, so its width is bounded by the grid column's own 100% by
construction rather than by measurement. Measured at 1200/992/700/320px: `.snapshot-status`
visible at every width, Refresh 36×36, `scrollWidth == clientWidth` throughout.

**FIND-04** — *"Each result shows a snippet with the match highlighted."* Both snippet defects
are fixed. `foldCase()` replaces the whole-string `toLowerCase()` with a code-point-wise fold
that substitutes only when UTF-16 length is unchanged, so a length-changing case mapping can no
longer shift every subsequent highlight range. The seed loop became three passes — seed in
absolute coordinates, sort and linear-merge, materialize once — so overlapping windows merge
rather than duplicating body text or cutting a match into an unhighlighted fragment.
