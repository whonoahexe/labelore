Let the user scan a folder such as ~/Work and pick the GSD projects in it (PLAT-05): a guarded POST
that only walks folders inside $HOME, two levels down, never through a symlink, capped and bounded
(D-15), and a second POST that remembers the picked projects through the same add path without
loading them (D-14, D-17). Nothing scans unless asked (D-16).

Purpose: the landing page's scan checklist in 08-13 calls these two routes; the containment rules
are what stop the scan becoming a directory-listing oracle (SEC-05).
Output: listChildEntries in local-fs.ts, the exported expandTilde, folder-scan.ts, the registry and
API additions, and their tests.
</objective>
