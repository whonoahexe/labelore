- Finding body: `--fs-4` (0.82rem), `max-width: 64ch`, `--lh-relaxed`, `--foreground`.
- Finding row: padding `--space-4` 0, hairline `1px solid var(--border)`, ordinal column `--space-8`, column gap `--space-3`; last row `padding-bottom: 0`.
- Ordinal: `--muted-foreground`, `--font-mono`, `--fs-2`, tabular-nums (never `--primary`).
- Bold: `--fw-semibold` inside the lead and finding body.
- Summary text gap kept at `--space-8` (user-set two-beat spacing from b34d25d).
